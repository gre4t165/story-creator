Story Creator generated sound effects (resources/sfx/generated)

Whooshes, swoosh, riser, boom, hit, drone, heartbeat, clock and the ambiences (rain, wind, ocean,
night, room tone) were synthesized with FFmpeg for Story Creator. They contain no recorded material.
